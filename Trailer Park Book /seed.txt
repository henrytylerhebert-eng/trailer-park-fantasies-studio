TRAILER PARK FANTASIES — a serialized Southern-Gothic mystery with a campy, fashionable
heart. Think trailer-park Sherlock Holmes by way of Louisiana bayou country: atmospheric
and slow-burn, but funny, warm, and full of characters who think they're living their
most glamorous life and have no idea how ridiculous they are. ("Trailer chic.")

HEROINE: Brooke "Bug" Barnett — a sharp, tattooed master detective with a signature bee
tattoo (hence "Bug"), a chunky turquoise statement necklace and cuffs, cowboy boots, and
an instinct for nonsense she can spot from three lots away. Southern charm over a steel
spine. She reads people the way other people read road signs. Her partner is a scruffy,
loyal little terrier who growls at liars and is right more often than the sheriff.

SETTING: Crystal's Cove Mobile Home Community — a rural Acadiana, Louisiana trailer park
at golden-hour dusk. String lights, lit-up trailers, a water tower, power lines, a hazy
bayou treeline, and a front-gate sign that lost its L and now reads "uxury Living at
Affordable Prices." Everyone treats the Cove like a luxury resort. It is not.

THE ENSEMBLE (eccentric residents who become Bug's found family — and her suspect pool):
- Crystal Mouton — park manager and self-crowned queen, runs the Cove like a lifestyle
  brand, monogrammed clipboard and bedazzled glasses; will protect her empire at any cost.
- Tammy Beaumont — bathtub-wine "vintner" and self-styled Barefoot Contessa of the Cove,
  knows everyone's business, trades secrets for favors.
- Dale Scruggs — self-appointed "Director of Perimeter Security," fourteen trail cams,
  tactical vest over a Hawaiian shirt, always leveraging something.
- Ricky Thibodaux — anxious backyard inventor whose autonomous lawnmower "Sheila" keeps
  turning up at crime scenes by accident.

INCITING MYSTERY: the disappearance of Bobby Boudreaux from Lot 5 — truck still parked,
TV still on, one garden gnome missing. Every resident has an alibi; none of them hold up.
Beneath the small-town misdirection lies a slow-burn land-grab conspiracy that threatens
to erase the Cove and everyone in it.

TONE & CRAFT: Southern-Gothic atmosphere fused with quirky humor and genuine emotional
weight. Heavy on misdirection and red herrings, slow-burn relationships, found-family
warmth, and characters quietly healing from past trauma. Emotional intensity in the vein
of Chloe Walsh's character-driven style (directional reference only — original prose).
Each installment: one self-contained case that advances the larger conspiracy arc.
