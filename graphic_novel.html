Introduction & Context

Welcome to Trailer Park Fantasies, a digital graphic novel crafted with the help of cutting‑edge AI writing agents and guided by the storytelling sensibilities of Irish author Chloe Walsh. This project blends innovative technology with heartfelt, human themes to create a unique reading experience set in a Southern‑Gothic trailer park.

AI Agents & Pipelines

We studied several open‑source agents designed for autonomous book creation. These systems employ specialized agents for tasks such as world building, character planning, drafting, editing and formatting. They run iterative revision loops and human review gates to maintain quality, and some even generate artwork, audio and marketing materials. Key takeaways include:

  • Multi‑agent orchestration: Projects like autonovel and the AutoGen Book Generator divide responsibilities among agents (world builder, story planner, writer, editor, memory keeper) and propagate changes across layers to keep the narrative consistent.
  • Revision & quality control: Deep revision cycles with AI beta readers and adversarial editing improve tone and pacing while keeping a human aesthetic.
  • World & character building: Tools like LibriScribe automate the creation of a world bible, character registry and chapter outlines to ensure continuity across chapters.
  • Publishing pipeline: Scripts for formatting and exporting to multiple formats (HTML, ePub, PDF) make it easy to adapt the story to different platforms.

Narrative Inspiration

Chloe Walsh’s novels are known for emotionally intense contemporary romances that blend humour and heartache. Common themes include found family, trauma recovery, slow‑burn relationships and small‑town community dynamics. These elements have informed our own storytelling choices:

  • Found family & community: Emphasize the trailer‑park residents banding together like Walsh’s characters, who find strength in their chosen families.
  • Healing from trauma: Explore characters’ backstories and personal battles, portraying recovery as non‑linear and grounded in supportive relationships.
  • Slow‑burn connections: Let romantic or deep friendships develop gradually through trust, humour and vulnerability.
  • Balancing light and dark: Blend humour and hope with serious subject matter to mirror Walsh’s ability to juxtapose comic relief with heavy themes.

Visual Style

The series adopts a bold cel‑shaded aesthetic reminiscent of Grand Theft Auto loading screens. Key characteristics include thick outlines, painterly gradients, semi‑realistic faces, high‑contrast lighting and a "trailer chic" palette of dusty rose, golden‑hour peach, turquoise pops, denim blues and deep plum shadows. Chapter 1 – Welcome to Crystal's Cove

The warm glow of string lights casts a dreamy haze over Crystal's Cove, a ramshackle trailer park deep in the Louisiana bayou. Brooke 'Bug' Barnett stands at the edge of the park, long coat whipping in the breeze, her turquoise necklace catching the golden hour light. Her scruffy terrier, Cricket, sits at her boots, ears perked, ready for the night's mysteries.

Bug: 'It feels too quiet tonight, Cricket. Stay sharp.'
